Real-World Scenario & System Overview

Students today want expert help beyond the classroom, but finding the right tutor, fixing a time and paying for sessions is still spread across calls and chats. An Online Tutoring System brings tutors, students, courses and live sessions onto one platform. It organises course content, schedules sessions, tracks learning progress and handles payments securely.

Object abstraction mirrors real-world learning:
  • A Course contains Modules; Modules contain Lessons.
  • Tutors and Students meet in scheduled Tutoring Sessions.
  • Every payment mode plugs in through one Payable interface.
Problem Statement & Core Objectives
Problem Statement

Running online learning well means keeping course content organised, matching students with tutors at the right time, and accepting payments in different ways, without the code breaking every time a new feature or payment method is added.

Key Objectives
Structured Course Content
A Course owns its Modules and Lessons, so content always stays complete and consistent.
Flexible Session Scheduling
Anything bookable follows one Schedulable contract for booking and cancelling slots.
Pluggable Payments
New modes like Card or UPI just implement Payable, with no change to existing code.
Composition and Interfaces
COMPOSITION (Strong Ownership)

  • Part depends on its whole
  • Shared lifecycle
  • Cascade deletion
  • No orphan modules or lessons

Our OOD Core Concept
Composition
  • Strong ownership between parent and child objects.
  • Child cannot exist without its parent.
  • Shared lifecycle and cascade deletion.
  • Used for: Course → Module, Module → Lesson.
Interfaces
  • A contract of methods with no body.
  • Classes implement it in their own way.
  • Callers depend on the contract, not the class.
  • Used for: Schedulable, Payable (Card, UPI).
Software Quality Assurance (QA) & OOD Impact
Lifecycle Safety
Composition prevents orphan modules and lessons, so course data stays consistent.
High Cohesion
Each class has one clear role: Course organises, Session schedules, Payment pays.
Extensibility
A new payment mode or bookable item only needs to implement an interface.
Testability
Interfaces allow mock payments and sessions, making unit tests simple and isolated.

Conclusion

By applying Composition and Interfaces, the Online Tutoring System keeps course content safely owned and consistent, while scheduling and payments stay flexible through clear contracts. This gives a clean, testable and easily extendable foundation for real-world e-learning.
